Amado is a companion app for the free Amado macOS menu bar agent. The companion requires macOS 15 or later and does not require an account or purchase.

Review steps:
1. On the iPhone setup screen, tap Get Amado for Mac or visit https://pangmo5.dev/Amado/.
2. Install and open the Mac app.
3. Choose Show pairing code in the Amado menu bar menu.
4. In the iPhone app, tap Scan pairing QR and scan the code.
5. The paired row shows the Mac's current Locked or Unlocked state. Pull to refresh if needed.
6. Tap the paired Mac to lock it. The app reports whether it was already locked or became locked.

The one-tap lock action is available in the iPhone app, Apple Watch app, Home Screen widget, and Control Center. Widget and Control Center actions update with progress or result feedback. The Home Screen widget also has a refresh button that reads the Mac's current lock state. Bluetooth proximity auto-lock is configured and performed by the Mac app, so it does not require the iPhone app to remain open.
