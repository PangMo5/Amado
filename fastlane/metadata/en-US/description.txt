Lock your Mac with one tap, or just walk away.

Amado gives you direct lock controls across your Apple devices. When you do not want to tap anything, Bluetooth proximity can lock the Mac automatically after you leave with your iPhone.

ONE TAP, EVERYWHERE

Lock a paired Mac from the iPhone app, Apple Watch, a Home Screen widget, or Control Center. See the Mac's current locked or unlocked state in the iPhone app, or refresh it directly from the widget. Every action reports whether the Mac was already locked, became locked, or could not confirm the transition.

WALK-AWAY AUTO-LOCK

Bluetooth proximity is a primary Amado experience. Smart detection learns the normal nearby signal, filters brief spikes, follows departure trends, and locks promptly when departure confidence is high. Choose Conservative, Balanced, or Fast sensitivity, or switch to Manual for direct RSSI controls. Keyboard and pointer activity cannot postpone a confirmed departure lock, and the iPhone app does not need to remain open.

LOCAL FIRST

Bonjour connects directly on your local network. For access away from home, Amado can use an HTTPS tunnel that you choose and operate. Amado does not require an account or hosted relay service.

SECURE PAIRING

Scan the QR code shown by the Mac app to pair. Commands and their matching responses are authenticated with HMAC-SHA256, fresh timestamps, and one-time nonces. Amado can lock your Mac and report its lock state. It cannot unlock it.

The free Amado macOS companion app is required. Download it from pangmo5.dev/Amado/ or GitHub.
